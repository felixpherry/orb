# Research

This folder is one research question, worked on by a group of Claude sessions.
Every thread in the group runs here and shares these files, so read what the
others have written before you start, and leave what you find for them.

## Where results go

Write findings to `notes/`, one Markdown file per topic. Cite a primary source
for every claim (official docs, source code, a spec, or a command you ran and
its output). Say what you couldn't verify.

## Falsifier round

Once a thread has written its claims, a second thread tries to falsify each
one: it looks for a source or an experiment that contradicts the claim. It
writes a verdict for every claim (holds, wrong, or unverified, with the
evidence) to `notes/verdicts.md`. Only claims that hold go into the answer.
