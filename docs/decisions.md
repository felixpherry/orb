| Stacked resize | Follow zellij 0.45's `stacked_resize` (`TiledPanes::stacked_resize_pane_with_id` in `zellij-server/src/panes/tiled_panes/mod.rs`). `Cmd +` tries the neighbours above, below, left and right in that order, skipping a side whose panes don't all sit within the focused pane's (or its stack's) edge and cover it. Towards the first one it grows 30% of the tab's height or width; when that would leave a pane there under 5 rows or 5 columns, frame included (zellij's `MIN_TERMINAL_HEIGHT`/`MIN_TERMINAL_WIDTH`), or under 5% of the tab, it stacks the focused pane with the panes touching it on that side, showing the focused pane.
